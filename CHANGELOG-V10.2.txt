RSR SHOP V10.2 — SEPARATED MOBILE CHECKOUT

- The long order form is now separated into clear steps:
  1. Method
  2. Roblox Account & Amount
  3. Game Pass Verification (CT/NCT only)
  4. Payment & Receipt
- Only one order step is displayed at a time.
- Sticky step progress remains visible while using a phone.
- Mobile Back and Next controls stay at the bottom of the screen.
- Instant and Gifting automatically skip the unnecessary Game Pass step.
- Buyers cannot move forward until required information in the current step is complete.
- Successful Game Pass verification provides a direct Continue to Payment button.
- Payment and receipt fields no longer require scrolling through every earlier order section.
- The checkout step is remembered temporarily during the same browser session.
- Added Filipino, Cebuano and Spanish wording for the new checkout controls.
- All V10.1 translations, notifications, security, admin tools, accounts and order systems are preserved.
