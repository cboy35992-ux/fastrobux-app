RRECK SHOPFAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need my Roblox password?

No. Never provide your password, verification code or authentication cookie.

Why must I create an account?

Your account protects access to order history, receipts, support messages and reviews.

How long is Instant stock reserved?

By default, 60 minutes while payment is reviewed. The administrator can change the server configuration.

Which payments are supported?

GCash, GoTyme/InstaPay, PayPal, Wise and Payoneer can be displayed. Confirm current international payment details with support before paying.

When can I review an order?

After the administrator marks it Completed.
