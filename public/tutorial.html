How to create a Roblox Game Pass

Required for Covered Tax and Not Covered Tax.

  1. Open the Roblox Creator Dashboard.
  2. Select the experience that belongs to your Roblox account.
  3. Open Monetization → Passes.
  4. Click Create a Pass, upload an icon and give it a name.
  5. Open the created Pass, go to Sales, and turn on Item for Sale.
  6. Enter the exact required Game Pass price shown by Reck Shop.
  7. Save the price, copy the Game Pass URL, and paste it into the order form.
  8. Click Verify Username & Game Pass. Do not change the price after it is verified.
Exact-price rule: If Reck Shop requires 1,429 Robux, the Game Pass must be exactly 1,429—not 1,428 or 1,430. A mismatched price blocks the transaction.
🛡️

Covered Tax (CT)

Best when you want to receive the full selected Robux amount after Roblox's marketplace fee.

  1. Select Covered Tax.
  2. Search and confirm your Roblox username.
  3. Enter the amount you want to receive.
  4. Create a Game Pass using the exact higher required price shown.
  5. Paste and verify the Game Pass link.
  6. Pay, upload the receipt and submit.
ExampleWant to receive: 1,000 RobuxRequired Game Pass: 1,429 Robux
💸

Not Covered Tax (NCT)

Lower payment price, but Roblox's marketplace fee is deducted from the Game Pass sale.

  1. Select Not Covered Tax.
  2. Search and confirm your Roblox username.
  3. Enter the Game Pass amount.
  4. Create the Game Pass at the exact required price.
  5. Paste and verify the Game Pass link.
  6. Pay, upload the receipt and submit.
ExampleGame Pass price: 1,000 RobuxExpected after Roblox fee: approximately 700 Robux
⚡

Robux Instant

Uses available shop stock for faster processing. “Instant” means the order can be processed quickly after payment approval; Roblox may still place proceeds in Pending Robux.

  1. Select Robux Instant.
  2. Confirm your exact Roblox username.
  3. Enter an amount within the available stock.
  4. Complete payment and upload the receipt.
  5. Wait for payment approval and delivery processing.
  6. Check your Roblox Pending Robux when applicable.
Important: Delivery timing depends on staff approval, stock and Roblox processing. The site should never promise guaranteed immediate availability.
🎁

In-Game Gifting

Used when a supported Roblox game allows an item to be gifted or purchased for another player.

  1. Select In-Game Gifting.
  2. Confirm your Roblox username.
  3. Enter the exact game name and exact item name.
  4. Pay and upload a clear receipt.
  5. Use private order chat to coordinate availability and delivery.
  6. Confirm the item received before the order is completed.
Availability rule: Gifting only works when the selected game and item support the required delivery method. Staff must confirm before promising delivery.

Receipt and order-status guide

A clear receipt prevents delays.

Receipt must show

  • Successful payment status
  • Correct amount
  • Date and time
  • Transaction/reference number
  • Sender name when available

Status meanings

  • Pending Payment Review — Receipt is waiting for checking.
  • Approved — Payment has been confirmed.
  • Processing — Delivery work has started.
  • Ready for Delivery — Final delivery action is ready.
  • Completed — Order is finished.
  • Declined — Information or payment did not pass checking.
